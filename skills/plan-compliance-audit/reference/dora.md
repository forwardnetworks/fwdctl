# DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554): what Forward can evidence

Read when an audit or question names DORA, the EU Digital Operational Resilience Act for financial entities. The regulation is at <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj/eng>; the detailed ICT risk management requirements are in Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 (<https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2024/1774/oj/eng>), which this page does not map: read it for the network security detail. The "what it asks" column is **our paraphrase** of the network-relevant part, not the regulation's wording. **The mapping to skills is ours, not a regulator's and not a lawyer's.** The wording of Articles 8(1), 8(2), 8(4), 8(6), 9(4)(b), (c), (e) and (f), 10(1), 11(5), 24 and 25(1) was read in the official EUR-Lex text on 2026-11-18; the other articles are cited by number and title only. Check the text at EUR-Lex before quoting any of it.

## How to use it

- DORA obliges a **financial entity**; no tool certifies compliance with it. Never answer "compliant with DORA". Answer which network-relevant requirements Forward can evidence, with what, and what the evidence does not show. Forward sees the network, not the entity's governance, business impact analysis, incident reporting or suppliers.
- **evidence** means Forward reads data that answers the network slice of the requirement. **partial** means part of it, or only configuration text, or judgment. A requirement not in the table is **not evidenced by Forward**: say so, and do not invent coverage.
- An "evidence" level holds only when the feature or check behind it exists in that organization and build (an org property that is off, a check nobody defined, a Preview route an older build lacks). `inspect-environment` (its features table, the rows with `topic: security`) says which of those features this organization has on, and for an off one what evidence is missing and who can change it; turning one on is `edit-org-property` (a dry run first). Say which was missing instead of reporting the requirement as met or not met.
- A check that returned no rows because nothing matched its scope is **unknown**, not met (see `check-network-compliance`). Name the snapshot the evidence is from.

## Network-relevant requirements

| Article | What it asks (our paraphrase) | Level | Skills and views | What the evidence does not show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8(1), 8(4) Identification | Identify, classify and document ICT assets, including network resources and remote sites; map the critical ones; map the configuration of assets and the links and interdependencies between them. | partial | `inspect-inventory` (kinds `devices`, `summary`, `interfaces`), `inspect-topology` (kinds `links`, `maps`), `plan-device-audit` | the network configuration and links of the devices Forward collects, as collected (the strongest fit in this table); classification, which assets are critical and the business functions they serve are not read (edge asset criticality is EXPERIMENTAL and off by default). |
| 8(2) Identification | Identify cyber threats and ICT vulnerabilities relevant to the assets, on a continuous basis. | evidence | `inspect-vulnerabilities` (network, one CVE, one device) | CVE matches by OS version (and some configuration); a patch not yet collected is invisible, a match is not exploitability, platforms without CVE analysis are not covered; "continuous" is a process. |
| 8(6) Identification | Keep the inventories and update them periodically and at every major change. | partial | `inspect-snapshots`, `inspect-history`, `compare-device-config` | the as-collected state over time and what changed between two snapshots; the register the entity maintains is not Forward. |
| 9(4)(b) Protection and prevention | Sound network and infrastructure management; isolate affected assets in a cyber-attack; design the network connection infrastructure so it can be severed or segmented to prevent contagion. | partial | `investigate-reachability`, `check-network-compliance` (isolation checks), `inspect-topology` kind `zones`, `plan-security-posture` | segmentation as configured and modelled, strong for what the model covers and silent on what it does not (unmodelled internet, unsupported devices); an isolation check can pass vacuously; the ability to sever "instantaneously" and any automated isolation mechanism are not evidenced. |
| 9(4)(c) Protection and prevention | Limit logical access to assets to what legitimate, approved functions need. | partial | `check-network-compliance` and `inspect-device-files` on ACL and firewall policy | access control on the network (ACLs, firewall rules); user and entitlement management elsewhere is not read. Security rules for PAN-OS and FortiOS in NQE are EXPERIMENTAL and off by default. |
| 9(4)(e) Protection and prevention | ICT change management, approved by management with specific protocols. | partial | `verify-change` (predict a change before it is made), `plan-change-review`, `edit-change-set`, `inspect-history` | the change itself and its predicted effect on reachability; approvals and the change process are not visible. Predict needs the org property PREDICT_MODELING. |
| 9(4)(f) Protection and prevention | Documented policies for patches and updates. | partial | `inspect-vulnerabilities`, `inspect-inventory` kind `lifecycle` | OS version, CVE exposure and OS support dates; whether patches are applied by policy, and software outside the network devices, are not read. |
| 10(1) Detection | Detect anomalous activities, including ICT network performance issues, and identify potential material single points of failure. | partial | `inspect-performance`; `investigate-reachability` (reference `reading-paths.md`, "two paths are not two failure domains": compare the hops of the paths for a shared device) | performance Forward polls and the shared devices on the paths between named endpoints; detecting anomalous activity as it happens is outside Forward, and a single point of failure is found only for the flows you ask about. |
| 11(5) Response and recovery | Design and use ICT assets and services in line with the business impact analysis, in particular with redundancy of all critical components. | partial | `investigate-reachability` (is there more than one path, and do the paths share a device) | redundancy of paths between named endpoints; which components are critical comes from the entity's analysis, and failover behaviour and timing are not simulated here. |
| 24, 25(1) Testing | A digital operational resilience testing programme with appropriate tests, which include vulnerability assessments and scans and network security assessments. | partial | `plan-security-posture`, `check-network-compliance`, `inspect-vulnerabilities` as inputs to a network security assessment | the network side of an assessment (exposure, segmentation, configuration against a baseline); the programme, its independence and its frequency are the entity's. |

Not evidenced by Forward: governance and the management body's duties (Articles 5 and 6), backups and restoration (Article 12: collected device configurations are a record of state, not a backup), incident management, classification and reporting (Articles 17 to 23: Forward can inform an investigation with `plan-incident-triage` and `plan-what-changed`, but holds no incident process), threat-led penetration testing (Article 26), third-party risk and the register of information (Articles 28 to 30), and information sharing. That judgment is ours and can be wrong in a particular network.

## Features that add evidence

Several rows depend on an organization property that can be off: advanced reachability and flow computation (the reachability rows), Predict (change management), the CVE configuration analysis and index updates (vulnerabilities), collector health, and the experimental security rules and threat exposure. `inspect-environment` reads them from the organization's configuration, with the license facets: the rows with `topic: security` say which are on and, for an off one, what evidence is missing and who may change it. Turning one on is a write to the organization (`edit-org-property`, a dry run first, `plan-safe-write`); a feature marked EXPERIMENTAL changes between builds, so use it to look, not as a standing dependency.
