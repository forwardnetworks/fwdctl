# find-nqe-query: inputs

`network_id` and one of `question` (plain words, to search), `query_id` (a `Q_` id: return that saved query's source) or `path` (the same, by library path), with optional `commit_id` to read it at a library commit instead of the head (the result names the commit; a path is looked up among the head's paths). Optional: `list: true` (browse instead of search: one level of the library as a tree, the directories under `directory`, default `/`, with how many queries are below each and the queries directly in it; use it to learn the top-level folders or whether a directory exists), `directory` (limit the library to a folder), `limit` (default 8,
at most 25).

**What a commit is.** `commit_id` on its own (no `question`, `query_id` or `path`; `head` is the head) says what that library commit last changed and who made it: the queries whose last change it was, plus its author, time
and title from the history of one of them. Forward has no list of library commits, so a commit that no query last changed in is **unknown**, not empty. The author is personal: keep it out of public places.

## Library-wide reads: recursive, intent, source_matches

- `list: true` (the tree): directories directly under `directory` with how many queries are below each, and the queries directly in it. One level only. Does not combine with `question` (use `intent` to filter a listing).
- `recursive: true` (with `list`, or alone): every query under `directory` at any depth, each with `path`, `query_id`, `intent` and `repository`, sorted by path; up to 200 are shown and the rest are counted in `omitted` (narrow `directory` or `intent`). The source is not read.
- `intent`: a regular expression (Go RE2; case-sensitive unless it starts with `(?i)`) matched against each query's `@intent` text. Alone, or with `list` or `recursive`, it gives the recursive listing of the matching queries. `^\s*$` finds the queries with no intent (empty or blank). A customer that keeps categories at the end of the intent as a stand-in for tags, such as `[tags: health-check]`, finds them with `tags:.*health-check`; anchor with `\]$` to match the end. With `question` it narrows the word matches to those queries.
- `source_matches`: a regular expression searched, line by line, in the SOURCE of the organization's saved queries (`Q_` ids; the built-in `FQ_` queries are counted but not read) under `directory` (default the whole library), optionally only those whose intent matches `intent`. A literal dot is `\.`: to find every read of a field, `\.negated\b`. Each match lists the query's path, id, intent, the number of matching lines and the first three of them (trimmed to 160 characters), up to 100 queries. Each source is one request, so at most 300 are read per call; when more qualify, or a read fails, the search is **partial**: it says how many were not searched, and a partial search with no match is **unknown**, never "none". `commit_id` reads the sources at that library commit (the listing is the head's). Does not combine with `question`, `list` or `recursive`. `commit_id` is refused with `recursive` or `intent` alone (they read the head's listing).
